Before you start Most babies begin solids around 6 months, when they sit with support and show interest in food. Check with your pediatrician first. No honey before age 1, no added salt or sugar, and cut foods to a safe size and texture for your baby's stage. Introduce common allergens (egg, peanut, fish) as your pediatrician advises.
